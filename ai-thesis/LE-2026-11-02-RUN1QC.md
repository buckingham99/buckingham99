## 1a. DE and UK par 30Y (pulled 11-02, computed)

Neither source publishes a daily 30Y par yield, so par is computed from the published zero-coupon curve: par coupon c = f × (1 − DF(30)) / Σ DF(tᵢ), with coupon frequency f matching the market (Bunds annual, gilts semi-annual) and DF(t) = exp(−z(t)·t) for continuously compounded zero rates.

| Date | DE zero 30Y (published) | DE par 30Y (computed, annual coupon) | UK spot 30Y (published) | UK par 30Y (computed, semi-annual coupon) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10-28 | 3.77 | 3.761 | 5.728 | 5.697 |
| 10-29 | 3.77 | 3.757 | 5.704 | 5.664 |
| **10-30** | **3.78** | **3.759** | **5.723** | **5.677** |
| 11-02 | 3.76 | 3.735 | not yet in file | — |

- **DE:** zero rates rebuilt from the six published Svensson parameters (BBSIS …ZST.B0/B1/B2/B3/T1/T2…) match the published 30Y zero to two decimals on every date (e.g. 10-30: 3.780 vs 3.78), so the parameter set is right. **Assumption:** the Svensson rates are continuously compounded (the standard form of the model); the Bundesbank methodology page could not be read because www.bundesbank.de still does not resolve. If they were annually compounded instead, 10-30 par would be 3.692 — a 7bp difference. Confirm once www.bundesbank.de is reachable.
