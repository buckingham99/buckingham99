## 2. What the article adds to the 11-08 report

1. **Damage extent: the report carries one side of a conflict.** The 11-08 report states "minor" (Energy Ministry) in §1, §4B and §8. AP, CNN-verified video and a NYT reporter describe heavy or severe damage to at least one floor. Neither side says the building's function was lost, and it is an **administrative building, not a production, processing or export asset**. The §5 escalation condition ("escalates if production or export assets are hit") is therefore **NOT MET** either way. The conflict should still be recorded instead of the single word "minor".
2. **The Houthi claim names an asset class that would matter: "Aramco oil storage tanks".** If verified, storage tanks are an oil-system asset and would bear on the §5 escalation line. It is **T3 and unverified**. No Saudi or Aramco statement mentions tanks, and the Houthi 10-24 claims against the East-West Pipeline, Yanbu and Rabigh were not borne out (10-25 report: Argus source says Yanbu and Rabigh undamaged). **Weight: NOISE until Aramco or the Energy Ministry address it.**
3. **Saudi Civil Defense alerts covered Dammam, Al Ahsa and Buqaiq** (Infobae relay, T2). Buqaiq is Abqaiq, the main crude processing site. An alert is not an attack. This is context for why the Eastern Province appears in the coalition statements.
4. **New maritime item not in the 11-08 report: the Khasab tanker fire (UKMTO 199-26).** The report's data gate has the Khor Fakkan incident (18nm E, bulk carrier) but not this one. Khasab (Musandam) sits on the Strait itself, which is closer to the IRGC "unauthorized route" mine claims than Khor Fakkan is. A UKMTO-logged tanker fire with crew evacuated is **the first series-of-record entry this weekend that is consistent with** the IRGC claims. It does **not** confirm a mine: UKMTO gives no cause. The 10-29/10-30 UKMTO incidents near Khasab were a projectile hit and a limpet-style charge, not mines.
5. **Tape context (no session):** Brent futures settled $98.32 on 11-06 (FMP, T2, context only; EIA spot is still at 11-03, $107.03). The weekend's events land at Monday's open. They do not count toward any OIL-SPOT-RULE clock.
