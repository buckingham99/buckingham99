## 2. What matters for the thesis (beyond the article)

1. **Demand signal: strong, and stated as supply-limited.** Q4 data center guide is +~50% q/q. For 2027 the company now expects revenue up **>80%**, server revenue roughly doubling, and data center above 80% of revenue. Su: "demand is higher than supply". AMD says it has secured "wafers, substrates, advanced packaging, memory" for much higher 2027 growth. These are forecasts, not enactments. They support Physical_AI_Demand 9 (held) without adding to it. Same treatment as the Sandisk CY2027 view.
2. **Quality flag: earnings far ahead of cash.** Q3 free cash flow was **$554M (4.1% of revenue)**, against $3.5B non-GAAP and $2.4B GAAP net income. Q2 FCF was $1,558M. Inventory rose ~$3B q/q to $11.5B. Capex was $648M. Total debt went from $3.2B to $7.1B, including the $4.75B August notes. Cash was $15.8B. Management calls this pre-building for the ramp. That reading is plausible, but it is not yet proven. **Watch:** whether Q4 FCF recovers as Helios ships. If inventory builds again with flat FCF, that points to channel or rack-acceptance timing risk.
3. **Customer-warrant accounting (circularity, contained).** GAAP EPS $1.46 includes a **$1.1B non-cash charge** for the warrants in "our previously announced customer agreement", recognised as MI450 ships. The CFO expects the charge to grow. The equity-for-volume structure is the vendor-financing pattern already logged under Quality_of_Marginal_AI_Demand. No share-count effect is expected in Q4. Zero weight, because the structure is known and the charge is non-cash. Note it as a Quality-side annotation, not new evidence.
4. **Consumer side weakening (adjacent, memory).** Client and gaming is guided down **>20% q/q** in Q4. Gaming is already −40% y/y on lower semi-custom. AMD lists memory supply as a risk factor. The primary documents do **not** attribute the client decline to memory pricing. For MEMORY-SCARCITY-01 this is **adjacent, unattributed, zero weight**. It pairs with Sandisk consumer NAND −29% q/q (LE-2026-11-01-SNDK).
5. **Tape (positioning, not evidence):** CNBC reports neoclouds (CoreWeave, Nebius) rallied on Tuesday and held gains after the print. AMD is still below its 50-day average ($548) after the late-October AI rout.
