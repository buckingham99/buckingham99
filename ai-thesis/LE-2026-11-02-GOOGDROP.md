## 2. What the article leaves out (material for the thesis)

1. **Alphabet is now issuing equity, not just "buying back less".** The piece frames buybacks in the past tense but never says the share count is rising. T1 on file: **no buybacks in 9M-26**, **$49.5B equity raised in June** (common + Berkshire-affiliate placement + mandatory convertible, capex named) and **$12.4B ATM sales in Q3** (LE-2026-10-30-04). This is the CAPEX-DEBT-01 Limb D fact pattern (pre-window per DR-2026-11-02-DANGLE G3).
2. **Off-balance-sheet support has grown.** $48.0B credit backstops on third-party data-center leases, Crux AI backstops up to $10.0B, $6.6B TPU repurchase obligations — all WATCH rows in TW-VF-GUARANTEE-01 v2.3, none accrued.
3. **Why the stock fell.** EPS miss (GAAP $2.12 vs ~$3.00, including a SpaceX mark-down and an EU fine), search revenue short for a second quarter, and FCF −$9.1B against ~−$2.7B expected (CNBC, Benzinga). The article attributes the move mostly to AI spend uncertainty.
